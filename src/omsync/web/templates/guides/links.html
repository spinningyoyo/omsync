{% endcall %} {% call section("contacts", "Contacts and balances") %}
  • The counterparty is who the money moved to or from.
  • Add your own names first. You are the owner.
  • omsync finds names as banks print them. Sam Park also fits SAM PARK, PARK SAM, and S PARK.
  • When more than one contact fits, omsync names nobody, and you choose.
  • A person with no contact shows by name, such as TAYLOR KIM. Save them as a contact to keep their payments together.
  • A contact's balance counts only Owed categories, such as Loans. In the demo, Priya borrowed $300, paid back $150, and owes $150.
