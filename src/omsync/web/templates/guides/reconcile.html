Some movements of money explain each other. A transfer leaves one of your accounts and arrives in another. A card payment leaves checking and clears the card. A payment goes to a person and, later, comes back from them. Reconciliation proposes a link for each of these pairs, and each link says what the movement was.

A link explains money. It never changes an amount the bank reported. And a false link costs more than a missing one: a wrong link can hide real spending, or misstate a reimbursement. So omsync asks you when it is unsure, and equal amounts alone are never enough.

After each import and each sync, omsync applies the proposals that need no review. These are the exact, unambiguous matches, and the queue labelled Auto holds them. They go on as one batch named as automatic, so you do not have to approve each one.

In the demo, every card payment and every savings transfer links this way, so the Review and Manual queues are empty.

Undo on that batch reverses its links, and the import stays. Those proposals come back, and the next import applies them again unless you unlink them one by one. A pair you unlink is remembered, so it is never proposed again.

Two queues wait for a decision:

{% call glossary("The queues") %} {{ term("Review", "Proposals that are less certain than the automatic ones. Read the evidence, then approve or reject each one.") }} {{ term("Manual", "Transactions you link yourself. Some show a proposed partner, and Link manually lets you choose the other transaction.") }} {% endcall %}

Each proposal shows its evidence: confirmation numbers, the signs, the dates, the amounts, and any account a description names. You can approve or reject one at a time. You can also select several, then use Approve selected or Reject selected. A rejected pair is not proposed again.

{% call callout("careful", "Rules that lose trust") %}

If you unlink two links that a rule made, that rule stops applying itself. Its later proposals move to Review, with a note that says why, and wait there for your decision.

Some money should have a partner and does not. A transfer or card payment with no partner is Not matched, and it is listed on Reconcile so it does not go unnoticed. A Zelle payment from a person is different: it is explained by the name, as the next paragraph describes.

When the other side is known, the row is explained instead, and it shows who it was with. A Zelle payment from a person with no saved contact is explained by the name as written, such as Other side: TAYLOR KIM. Saving that person as a contact stores their payments with the contact at once. The explained list is collapsed by default, and each row links to its transaction, so you can change its other side.
